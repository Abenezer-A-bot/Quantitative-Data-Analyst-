Core Quantitative Capabilities

  • Survey & questionnaire design: sampling strategy, power calculation, and sample size planning
  • Data preparation: data cleaning, variable recoding, outlier screening, and missing-data handling
  • Hypothesis testing: descriptive statistics, independent & paired t-tests, ANOVA, chi-square (χ2), correlation, and multiple regression

  • Clear statistical reporting: publication-ready tables, figures, and plain-language interpretation of what the findings mean

Constructive Reader Feedback

Alpha reader and beta reader

Authors often need an attentive, articulate reader before line-by-line editing begins. I read your draft as your intended audience would and deliver a structured diagnostic report.

Alpha reading

Early Drafts

For foundational or first-complete drafts. I evaluate the big picture: the central thesis or premise, structural arc, pacing, gaps in logic, and whether each chapter earns its place. You receive a detailed editorial letter with actionable structural recommendations.

  • Macro-structure and argument evaluation
  • Chapter-by-chapter developmental notes
  • Identification of missing evidence or redundancy

Beta reading

Near-Final Drafts

For polished drafts prior to final copyediting or publication. I read from the perspective of your target reader and flag confusing passages, weak transitions, tone shifts, and moments where momentum drops—so you know exactly how the finished book or monograph will land.

  • Clarity, pacing, and reader-engagement feedback
  • Passage-level margin reactions
  • Holistic reader-experience summary

Transparent Workflow

How a project runs

  1. Brief You share your dataset, research instruments, or manuscript along with your goal, style guide, and deadline. We agree on a clear scope and timeline.
  2. Work I run the statistical models in R or SPSS, or edit your manuscript using Word Tracked Changes and marginal comments.
  3. Review You inspect the draft tables, scripts, or edited chapters and ask questions. I walk you through the findings and revise until everything fits your purpose.
  4. Deliver You receive the final cleaned dataset, reproducible syntax, publication-ready tables, clean manuscript, and a concise change log.
